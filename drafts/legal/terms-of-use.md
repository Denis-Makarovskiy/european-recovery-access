## Who we are

This website is operated by MK REHAB DOO ("European Recovery
Access"). We are an independent placement and admissions coordination service:
we help families identify European addiction-treatment programs, check
availability, and coordinate admission, travel and family communication.

## What we are not

- **We are not a medical provider.** All clinical services — assessment,
  detoxification, treatment — are provided by the independent treatment
  programs you may be admitted to, under their own licenses and terms.
- **We are not an emergency service.** If someone is in immediate danger,
  call 911 or your local emergency number.
- **Nothing on this site is medical advice.** Information here is general and
  does not replace consultation with a qualified clinician.

## No guarantees

Program availability and admission depend on clinical suitability, travel
readiness and provider capacity. We do not guarantee admission within any
specific timeframe, and we do not guarantee treatment outcomes or recovery.
Any figures on this site describe our coordination process, not clinical
results.

## Costs

Treatment costs are set by the treatment programs; coordination fees are ours.
You receive the applicable program and coordination costs in writing before
making any commitment. Treatment is private-pay; insurance reimbursement, if
any, is a matter between you and your insurer.

## Your responsibilities

You confirm that information you submit is accurate and that you are legally
entitled to share the information you provide about another person. Travel
documents, visas and fitness to travel remain the traveller's responsibility;
we assist with coordination but cannot guarantee entry to any country.

## Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, our liability in connection with this
website and our coordination services is limited to the coordination fees you
have paid us. We are not liable for acts or omissions of treatment programs,
carriers or other third parties.
