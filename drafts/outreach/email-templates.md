# Outreach templates — professional referral development

Owner-approved copy source for direct outreach. One recipient per email, sent
personally — never a bulk blast from the recoveryeurope.com domain (it would
poison deliverability of admissions@). One follow-up maximum, 7–10 days later.

**Legal guardrails, non-negotiable:**
- US recipients: CAN-SPAM — truthful sender and subject, the Podgorica postal
  address in the footer, and an honoured opt-out line. B2B cold email is legal.
- Canadian recipients: CASL requires consent — do NOT cold-email Canada.
  Reach Canadian professionals via LinkedIn messages instead.
- Never offer or imply payment for referrals, in any wording. It is patient
  brokering — criminal in Florida, California and other states, and an ethics
  violation for attorneys and clinicians everywhere.
