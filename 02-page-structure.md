# 2. Page Structure

## Desktop frame

- Artboard width: 1440 px.
- Maximum content width: 1240 px.
- Side margins: 100 px at 1440 px viewport.
- Grid: 12 columns.
- Gutter: 24 px.
- Section vertical spacing: 104–128 px.

## 1. Header

Height: 80 px.

Left:

- Shield/route-style brand mark.
- European Recovery Access wordmark.

Center navigation:

- Why Europe.
- How It Works.
- What's Included.
- FAQ.

Right:

- Lock icon and “100% Confidential.”
- Text link: “Call Admissions.”
- Optional CTA button on wide screens: “Check Availability.”

Behavior:

- Transparent or white at page load depending on hero treatment.
- Sticky after scroll.
- White background with subtle bottom border after 24 px scroll.

## 2. Hero

Desktop minimum height: 720 px.

Background:

- Calm European clinical or residential setting.
- Mountain, lake or countryside environment acceptable.
- No visible alcohol, nightlife, beach-club, spa or vacation cues.
- Dark navy overlay from left and light darkening across the full image.

Two-column layout:

- Left: 7 columns.
- Right: 5 columns.

Left content:

- Eyebrow.
- H1.
- Supporting copy.
- Three concise proof bullets.
- Primary CTA.
- Microcopy.

Right content:

- Consultation form card.
- Card width: 440–480 px.
- Navy surface or white translucent surface.

Recommended form fields:

- Full name.
- Phone number.
- Email.
- Who needs treatment?
- How urgent is the situation?
- Submit.

## 3. Trust Strip

Immediately below hero.

Four short items:

- Independent placement service.
- English-speaking coordination.
- Private-pay access.
- US and Canada support.

Use icons and compact text. Avoid logos of organizations unless authorized.

## 4. Why Families Choose Europe

Heading and short introduction.

Four equal cards:

1. Faster access.
2. A new environment.
3. Fewer familiar triggers.
4. One coordinator.

Each card:

- Circular icon.
- 20–24 px title.
- 2–3 lines of body text.

## 5. Interactive Assessment

Large elevated card with two-column internal layout.

Left column:

- Section title.
- Explanation.
- Confidentiality note.

Right column:

- Multi-step assessment.
- Progress indicator.
- Selectable answer chips.
- Back and Continue buttons.

Steps:

1. Substance or primary concern.
2. Urgency.
3. Patient age.
4. Location and contact details.

After submission:

- Confirmation state.
- Option to schedule a call.
- Alternative: request a callback.

## 6. How It Works

Five-step horizontal timeline on desktop:

1. Confidential consultation.
2. Initial assessment.
3. Treatment matching.
4. Travel coordination.
5. Admission and handoff.

Each step includes:

- Number.
- Icon.
- Title.
- Short description.

## 7. What's Included

Seven compact items or cards:

- Initial case review.
- Program matching.
- Availability confirmation.
- Medical-record coordination.
- Airport pickup.
- Private transfer.
- Family communication and aftercare planning.

## 8. Why Trust the Process

Use process evidence, not invented outcomes.

Suggested metrics only if operationally true:

- Response within one business hour.
- Admissions support across North American time zones.
- Multiple European partner programs.
- One assigned coordinator.

If numbers are not yet proven, use qualitative trust statements instead.

## 9. Who This Is For

Two-column section.

Suitable when:

- The patient may accept treatment now.
- Local waiting times are a problem.
- The family wants a different environment.
- Private-pay care is possible.

May not be suitable when:

- Emergency medical stabilization is required before travel.
- The patient cannot legally travel.
- The family expects involuntary confinement.
- The patient needs a service unavailable within the partner network.

## 10. Testimonials

MVP recommendation:

Do not publish fabricated testimonials.

Until real, consented testimonials exist, replace the section with:

- “What families usually need help with.”
- Three anonymized scenario cards clearly marked as examples, not client stories.

## 11. FAQ

Two-column accordion on desktop.

Topics:

- Speed of admission.
- Cost.
- Insurance.
- Clinical assessment.
- Patient leaving treatment.
- Family involvement.
- Medication and detox.
- Travel documents.
- Program length.
- Privacy.

## 12. Final CTA

Dark navy section with calm background image or subtle gradient.

Headline:

“Your family may only get one clear window for treatment.”

Supporting line:

“We help you understand the options and move quickly when the time is right.”

Buttons:

- Check Availability.
- Call Admissions.

## 13. Footer

Columns:

- Brand and short descriptor.
- Navigation.
- Contact.
- Legal.

Mandatory disclaimer:

European Recovery Access is an independent placement and coordination service. It is not an emergency service and does not provide emergency medical care. Program availability and admission depend on clinical suitability and provider capacity.

## Mobile layout

Breakpoints:

- 375 px reference mobile frame.
- 768 px tablet.
- 1024 px small desktop.
- 1280 px desktop.

Mobile rules:

- Header becomes logo + menu + call icon.
- Hero becomes one column.
- Hero form is replaced by a button opening a full-screen or bottom-sheet form.
- H1 target size: 44–50 px.
- Cards stack vertically.
- Assessment options use full-width or two-column chips.
- Timeline becomes vertical.
- FAQ becomes one column.
- Sticky bottom bar: “Call” and “Check Availability.”
