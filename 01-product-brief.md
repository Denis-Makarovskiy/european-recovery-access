# 1. Product Brief

## Product name

**European Recovery Access**

Working tagline:

**When treatment can't wait.**

## Category

Independent European addiction-treatment placement and admissions coordination service.

Do not describe the product primarily as “medical tourism.” The service is positioned as a fast-access operator for families facing a narrow window of willingness to enter treatment.

## Target audience

Primary decision-makers:

- Parents of adult children with substance-use disorders.
- Spouses and partners.
- Adult siblings.
- Family attorneys and family-office representatives.
- Interventionists and private clinicians.

Geography:

- United States.
- Canada.

Typical situation:

- The loved one has agreed, or may briefly agree, to treatment.
- Local programs are unavailable, have a waiting list, are unsuitable, or feel too close to the patient's existing environment.
- The family is able to use private-pay treatment.
- The family wants a coordinated solution rather than researching programs alone.

## Core value proposition

European Recovery Access helps families identify and arrange admission to an appropriate European addiction-treatment program, often within days, while coordinating assessment, placement, travel, transfer and family communication.

## MVP hypothesis

The MVP tests whether visitors will:

1. Begin the assessment.
2. Complete the assessment.
3. Submit contact details.
4. Book or request a confidential call.
5. Answer an admissions call.

## Primary CTA

**Check availability**

## Secondary CTA

**Speak with Admissions**

## Key benefits

1. Faster access than many local alternatives.
2. Separation from familiar triggers, suppliers and routines.
3. One coordinator for treatment matching and travel logistics.
4. Private-pay simplicity.
5. English-speaking admissions support.
6. Confidential, discreet process.

## Important messaging constraints

Never claim:

- Guaranteed recovery.
- A fixed success rate without evidence.
- That a patient cannot leave.
- That admission is always possible within 24 or 48 hours.
- That all programs are medically appropriate for every patient.
- That the service provides medical advice before assessment.

Preferred wording:

- “Admission may be available within days.”
- “We coordinate placement based on clinical and practical fit.”
- “Distance from familiar surroundings may support continued engagement.”
- “Availability depends on clinical suitability, travel readiness and program capacity.”

## Brand personality

- Calm.
- Competent.
- Discreet.
- Decisive.
- Human.
- Premium, but not luxurious or glamorous.

Avoid:

- Resort imagery.
- Vacation language.
- Fear-heavy copy.
- Aggressive sales tactics.
- Claims that imply confinement.
