## Typography

### Primary sans-serif

**Inter**

Use for navigation, body, buttons, labels, forms and UI.

### Display serif

**Source Serif 4** or **Playfair Display**

Use only for hero headline and selected section headlines.

Preferred production combination:

- Source Serif 4.
- Inter.

## Borders

- Default: 1 px solid `#E1E6EB`.
- Interactive: 1 px solid `#C9D0D8`.
- Selected: 1.5 px solid `#B9893D`.
- Focus: 3 px outer focus ring using the focus color.

## Iconography

- Style: clean outline.
- Stroke width: 1.75–2 px.
- Rounded ends.
- Primary icon color: Navy 800.
- Accent details: Gold 500.

Recommended icon libraries:

- Lucide.
- Phosphor.

Do not mix icon libraries.

## Photography direction

Use:

- Modern European residential-clinical architecture.
- Quiet interiors.
- Mountain, forest or lakeside context.
- Natural daylight.
- Authentic, non-modelled human moments only when licensed.

Avoid:

- Medical staff posing with crossed arms.
- White-coat stock photography.
- Beaches, pools, yachts or resort cues.
- Images of visibly intoxicated or distressed people.
- Restraint, locked doors or confinement symbolism.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast minimum.
- Minimum body text 16 px on mobile.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Buttons minimum 44 px high.
- Form errors described in text, not color only.
- All icons with accessible labels where needed.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
